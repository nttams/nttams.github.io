Modeled, not measured. Each request costs 5 ms of CPU in a worker (parse and match), then 3 trips through the event loop (to augmenters, to bidders, back to the worker). One loop message costs 80 µs with logging inside the loop (72 µs without), and every extra worker thread adds 2.5% to that cost (cache and lock contention). Augmenters take 8 to 16 ms and bidders 14 to 26 ms. The loop discards messages of requests that are already late, which is cheap.
